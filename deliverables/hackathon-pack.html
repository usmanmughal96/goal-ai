The Problem

Sports games require expensive hardware or complex setup

Traditional sports simulations need controllers, motion sensors, or VR headsets. The barrier to entry is high. Meanwhile every laptop ships with a camera — and nobody uses it intelligently for gaming.

The Solution

Your webcam IS the controller

GOAL AI turns any laptop camera into a precision sports input device. MediaPipe detects 21 hand landmarks in real time. A thumbs-up gesture fires the shot; your wrist position sets the aim. Google Gemini 2.0 Flash calls the match live with AI commentary after every kick.

Playcast Recording Tips

  • Record at 1280×720 minimum. The game canvas looks best at full browser width.
  • Make sure your hand is well-lit for the webcam tracking section — dim hands = bad tracking footage.
  • Don't cut away during Gemini commentary — hold the shot so judges clearly read the AI text. It's your biggest differentiator.
  • Burn text overlays into the video (not as subtitles) so they show even when muted. Most people watch muted.
  • Add captions/subtitles for accessibility and muted autoplay on social.
  • YouTube title for max reach: "World Cup 2026 Penalty Kicks using just your Webcam + AI (Google Gemini)"
  • Share on Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and any dev communities. Phase 2 score = 7-day social engagement.
