## 📝 RECORDING TIPS

- **Screen resolution:** Record at 1280×720 minimum. The game canvas looks best at full-width.
- **Lighting:** Make sure your hand is well-lit for the webcam tracking shot.
- **Gemini commentary:** Wait for the text to appear on screen before cutting — it's the key differentiator, make sure judges see it clearly.
- **Keep energy high:** Cut dead air. The 1-minute demo should feel fast and exciting.
- **Captions:** Add burned-in text overlays for key moments so it works with sound off (judges often watch muted).
- **YouTube upload:** Set visibility to Public before submitting. Title: *"GOAL AI — Webcam Gesture Football · Google Gemini · Stanford Hackathon 2025"*
