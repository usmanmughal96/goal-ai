The Problem

Sports games require expensive hardware or complex setup

Traditional sports simulations need controllers, motion sensors, or VR equipment. The barrier to entry is high, and none use real gesture intelligence. There's a gap for zero-hardware, AI-native sports experiences.

The Solution

Your webcam IS the controller

GOAL AI transforms any laptop camera into a precision sports input device. Powered by MediaPipe for real-time hand tracking and Google Gemini for live AI match commentary, it delivers an arcade football experience entirely in the browser — no installs, no hardware, no latency.

⚽Game + Gemini

MediaPipe Hands tracks 21 3D landmarks at ~8 FPS via WebAssembly. The thumbs-up gesture fires a shot aimed by wrist position. After every kick, Gemini generates a live one-line commentary via the Gemini 2.0 Flash API.

Key Features
  • Thumbs-up gesture detection via 21-point landmark geometry
  • Real-time aim tracking with 60 FPS canvas rendering
  • AI goalkeeper with 3 difficulty tiers (easy → hard)
  • Gemini 2.0 Flash live commentary after every shot
  • Procedural stadium audio via Web Audio API (zero audio files)
  • Optical-flow fallback — works without webcam
  • Fully browser-native — no install, no server
